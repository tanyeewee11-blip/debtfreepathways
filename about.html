About DebtFreePathways

Welcome to DebtFreePathways.com, an independent financial education and calculation platform committed to helping individuals regain financial independence.

Our Mission

Managing debt can feel overwhelming. Our mission is to make personal debt elimination transparent, actionable, and mathematically accessible. We build clean, reliable, and privacy-first calculation tools powered by proven debt management methodologies, such as the Debt Snowball and Debt Avalanche strategies.

Methodology & Transparency

Our tools are engineered around standard annuity amortization formulas and rigorous mathematical models. All calculations are executed client-side inside your browser—we do not collect, store, or transmit your sensitive financial inputs or credit figures.

Editorial & Independent Review

All debt payoff strategies, repayment timelines, and financial guides hosted on this site are regularly reviewed against guidelines established by credible financial educational bodies (including the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau - CFPB). We strive to provide transparent information so users can make informed choices.
